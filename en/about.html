About Us

Zhengzhou XianDa Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. · Founded in 1999

Founded in 1999, Zhengzhou XianDa Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is a China-based manufacturer specializing in R&D and production of medical metrology instruments, parking-timing verification equipment, and communication metrology devices.

Working with universities and metrology authorities, we develop leading verification instruments and solutions for metrology departments, hospitals, and equipment manufacturers.
