About Advanced Tech

Medical device test equipment · Est. 1999 · Zhengzhou, China

Advanced Tech (Zhengzhou Xianda Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.) designs and manufactures portable simulators and calibrators for patient monitors, ECG equipment, and non-invasive blood pressure devices.

Since 1999 we have worked with universities, metrology institutes, hospitals, and equipment manufacturers to develop practical test tools for biomedical engineering and quality assurance teams. Our products are used across China for performance verification, incoming inspection, and routine PM testing.
