BPV5 NIBP Simulator

Portable non-invasive blood pressure simulator for dynamic NIBP waveforms and static pressure verification.

Overview

BPV5 is a NIBP simulator and calibrator for patient monitors, automatic blood pressure monitors, and related devices. It provides dynamic blood-pressure simulation and static pressure tests in one compact instrument—successor to our earlier BPV series with more than a decade of NIBP test-equipment experience behind it.

The design follows oscillometric blood-pressure measurement principles, using electromechanical control for repeatable cuff pressure profiles.

Key features

  • 7-inch color touchscreen and lithium battery — integrated, lightweight, with carry handle.
  • Dynamic NIBP simulation — set systolic, diastolic, and pulse rate independently.
  • Internal or external cuff selection for different monitor types.
  • Inflation and deflation pressure control modes.
  • Static pressure test sets — four customizable pressure points, saved for repeat use.
  • Multi-point auto measurement — one key runs a full static pressure sweep.
  • Automated test programs for common verification workflows (details on request).
  • Flexible positioning — flat, tilted, or upright on the bench.

Applications

  • NIBP module on patient monitor
  • Automatic blood pressure monitor
  • Incoming inspection and periodic maintenance
