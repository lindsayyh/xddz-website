ECG-6 Calibrator

Handheld ECG / EEG / patient-monitor ECG channel tester with touchscreen workflow and battery power.

Overview

The ECG-6 is a portable calibrator for ECG machines, digital EEG systems, and the ECG channels of patient monitors. It outputs programmed test signals for field verification and routine quality checks, with a touchscreen interface designed for technicians who work on the ward—not only in the lab.

Products in this family have been refined over two decades of ECG test-equipment development. ECG-6 adds a large touchscreen, rechargeable battery, and expanded waveform library in a handheld housing.

Key features

  • Handheld 7-inch touchscreen with friendly menus and shortcut keys to switch test items quickly.
  • Rich waveform library — square, sine, triangle, ECG simulation, heart-rate, and pacing-related signals (dozens of test waveforms).
  • Multi-purpose electrode terminals adapt to common ECG leads and patient-monitor connections.
  • Built-in calculators for tolerance and parameter math at the test site.
  • Configurable output paths — polarization voltage, simulated skin impedance, and input impedance can be switched in or out for different test setups.
  • One-key auto test runs a full sequence of required outputs in regulation order.
  • High-capacity battery (≥11,000 mAh) for extended field use; protective case included.

Applications

  • ECG recorders and electrocardiographs
  • Ambulatory (Holter) ECG systems
  • Digital EEG instruments
  • Patient monitor ECG channels
