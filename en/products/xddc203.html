XDDC203 Patient Simulator

Three routine tests in two minutes — simultaneous ECG, NIBP, and SpO₂ simulation for multi-parameter monitor QA.

Overview

The XDDC203 is a portable vital signs simulator for performance testing and quality control of multi-parameter patient monitors. It simulates electrocardiogram (ECG) signals, non-invasive blood pressure (NIBP), peripheral oxygen saturation (SpO₂), respiration, and end-tidal carbon dioxide (EtCO₂, optional).

Once the patient monitor is connected, complete routine ECG, NIBP, and SpO₂ performance checks in under two minutes — a workflow inspired by industry-leading all-in-one patient simulators, in a compact form factor built for Chinese hospitals and calibration laboratories.

Key features

  • Simultaneous simulation. ECG, NIBP, and SpO₂ run independently at the same time so you verify three monitor parameters in one setup.
  • 7-inch touchscreen. View and adjust ECG, NIBP, and SpO₂ settings together on one screen.
  • Pre-installed SpO₂ R-curves. Ten manufacturer R-curves including BCI, Nellcor, Masimo, HP (Philips), OxiMax, GE-Ohmeda, and Mindray — select the brand that matches the pulse oximeter under test.
  • ~8 hours battery life. Removable lithium-ion pack recharges via USB-C for field work.
  • Respiration simulation. Impedance-based respiration waveforms with adjustable rate and I:E ratio.
  • Optional modules. EtCO₂ breath simulation, temperature, invasive blood pressure (IBP), and cardiac output.
  • Parallel testing. Change output parameters while multiple functions run to shorten PM routines.
  • One-key sequences. Run pre-built performance verification programs (documentation available on request).

What’s included

  • XDDC203 vital signs simulator
  • SpO₂ artificial finger
  • NIBP test hose and adapters
  • NIBP cuff mandrel
  • Carrying case
